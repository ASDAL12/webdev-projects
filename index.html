Pugaogao Cold Spring

Pugaogao Cold Spring, also affectionately known as Abot Cold Spring, is a true hidden treasure tucked away in Salpad, Pasuquin, Ilocos Norte. Its name comes from the local dialect, describing how pure, crystal-clear water bubbles endlessly from deep subterranean chambers. Cherished by villagers for generations as a quiet natural sanctuary, this sacred spring now warmly opens its embrace to travelers seeking peaceful nature.

And it is even more breathtaking than its story! The water is strikingly clear—resembling liquid glass shimmering beneath the sun, revealing smooth river stones resting quietly along the bottom. Naturally ice-cold all year round, every dip feels like a gentle, refreshing hug on a warm tropical day. Free of loud music or commercial clutter, it preserves an authentic, serene beauty that invites you to breathe deeper and linger longer.

More than just a place to visit, Pugaogao is a haven to feel refreshed in both body and spirit. Whether you come to take a revitalizing swim, rest under the shaded forest canopies, or simply enjoy the tranquil sounds of flowing water, you will leave feeling lighter, cooler, and already looking forward to your next visit.

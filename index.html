Spot 01 / 03

Dos Hermanos Islands

📍 Coastal Pasuquin, Ilocos Norte

Experience the iconic twin limestone monoliths rising from the crystal-clear coastal waters of Pasuquin. Renowned for dramatic cliffside viewpoints, tidal sea pools, and breathtaking golden-hour sunsets, it is the premier photography and coastal sight of the municipality.

🌊 Twin Rock Formations 📸 Sunset View Deck 🏖️ Tidal Sea Pools
Spot 02 / 03

Salpad Waterfalls & River Trek

📍 Brgy. Salpad, Pasuquin

A hidden paradise nestled into lush mountain trails and bamboo canopies. Cool off in refreshing natural freshwater springs, hike along gentle riverside boulder paths, or enjoy traditional open-air bamboo picnic cottages with panoramic mountain horizons.

🏊 Cold Natural Spring 🌿 Riverside Eco Trail 🧺 Forest Cottages
Spot 03 / 03

Pasuquin White Sand Beach & Dunes

📍 Poblacion Coastline, Pasuquin

Unwind along expansive coastal shorelines where rolling sand dunes meet clear turquoise waves. Famous for fresh local sea-salt harvesting, scenic coastal breeze, and peaceful strolls undisturbed by heavy tourist crowds.

🏖️ Peaceful Shoreline 🧂 Local Salt Making 🌅 Morning Strolls

Traveler's Guide

Essential tips for tourists planning their trip.

📍 Location & How to Get There

Accessible via Laoag International Airport, followed by a scenic 30-minute northbound drive along the scenic Ilocos highway.

☀️ Best Time to Visit

November through May for sunny beach weather, tranquil seas, and clear mountain trekking trails.

🎟️ Entrance Fees & Permits

Free beach access. Eco-tourism environmental registration fee of ₱20-₱50 applies at selected protected waterfalls.

📞 Tourism Office Contact

Pasuquin Municipal Tourism Assistance: tourism@pasuquin.gov.ph | +63 (077) 123-4567.
